Football Leagues Directory

Browse football leagues by country and access league tables with a single click

How to Use This Directory

1. Click on any country to expand its league list

2. Click on a league name to open its table/standings page

3. To add your own links, replace the href="#" in each league item with your actual URL

Example: For Premier League tables, you would replace href="#" with href="https://s5.sir.sportradar.com/bet9ja/en/1/season/130281/standings"
